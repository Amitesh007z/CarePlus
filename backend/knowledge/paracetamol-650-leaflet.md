# Paracetamol 650 mg tablet

## What it is used for

Paracetamol is an over-the-counter tablet used to help reduce fever and mild pain.

## How to take it

Follow the dose on the pack and the advice of your pharmacist. Do not take another paracetamol-containing product at the same time. Do not take more than the label allows in 24 hours.

## When to stop and seek help

Stop taking it and speak to a doctor if the fever lasts longer than 3 days, if pain does not improve, or if you develop a skin rash.

## Warnings

Do not use this medicine if you have a known allergy to paracetamol. Keep out of reach of children.
