# Delivery

## Where we deliver

Orders are delivered to addresses inside India that are saved in your account. Delivery is not available to post-office boxes.

## Delivery time

Standard delivery takes 2 to 4 working days after payment. Orders placed after 6 pm are processed the next working day.

## Delivery charges

Delivery is free for orders of INR 499 or more. For smaller orders a flat delivery charge is shown at checkout before you pay.
