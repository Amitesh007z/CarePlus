# Dextromethorphan cough syrup

## What it is used for

This syrup is an over-the-counter cough medicine used to help relieve a dry, tickly cough.

## How to take it

Measure the dose with the cup or spoon supplied. Follow the dose on the label. Do not take it more often than the label says.

## Do not use if

Do not use this syrup if you are taking medicines called MAO inhibitors, or if you have a known allergy to any of its ingredients.

## When to seek help

See a doctor if the cough lasts longer than a week, if you have a fever, or if you cough up blood.
