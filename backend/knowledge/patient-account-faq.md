# Account and health data

## Why do I need to give consent?

CarePulse uses your health information, such as allergies, to check medicine suggestions and to share records with the doctors you consult. You can grant or withdraw consent in your profile at any time.

## What happens when I withdraw consent?

After you withdraw consent, CarePulse stops using your health information for new suggestions. Existing appointment and prescription records are kept as required by law.

## How do I change my profile?

Open your profile from the top menu, edit the fields you need, and save.
