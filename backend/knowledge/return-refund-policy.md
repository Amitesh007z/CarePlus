# Returns and refunds

## Eligibility

Unopened over-the-counter medicines in their original sealed pack can be returned within 7 days of delivery. Opened packs, and medicines that need a prescription, cannot be returned for safety reasons.

## Refund timing

Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method within 7 working days of the return being received and checked. Refunds for cancelled orders that were not shipped are processed within 3 working days.

## How to request a return

Open the order in your account, choose the item, and select Request return. A CarePulse team member will confirm the request by email. Do not send medicines back before you receive that confirmation.
