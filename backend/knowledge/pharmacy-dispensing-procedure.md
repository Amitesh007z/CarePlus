# Pharmacy dispensing procedure (staff only)

## Before dispensing a prescription

Check that the prescription is issued, unexpired and not already fulfilled. Check the patient's allergy record. If there is a conflict, stop and escalate to the pharmacist in charge.

## Stock

Reserve stock only for orders that are confirmed. Record every stock movement with a reason.

## Escalation

Any question about a prescription that is not clear is referred to the prescribing doctor before the order is released.
