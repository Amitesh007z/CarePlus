# Clinician triage protocol (internal)

## Purpose

This protocol is for clinicians only. It describes how a clinician records the reason for a visit and who should be seen first. It is not a patient-facing triage tool.

## Escalation

When a clinician sees a patient with chest pain, breathing difficulty or signs of stroke, they must follow the local emergency pathway at once and record the time of escalation.

## Documentation

Record the presenting complaint, duration and vital signs before any advice is given. Addenda are recorded as new entries, never as edits.
