# Ibuprofen 400 mg tablet

## What it is used for

An over-the-counter tablet used to help reduce mild pain and inflammation, such as headache or muscle ache.

## How to take it

Follow the dose on the pack. Take it with food if it upsets your stomach. Do not take more than the label allows in 24 hours.

## Do not use if

Do not use it if you have a known allergy to ibuprofen or similar pain medicines, or if you have had stomach bleeding.

## When to seek help

Speak to a doctor if the pain lasts more than 3 days, if you notice black stools, or if you develop a rash.
