# Product warranty

## What is covered

Non-medicine health devices bought through CarePulse carry a 24-month manufacturer warranty against defects. This replaces the 12-month term from version 1.

## Medicines

Medicines are not covered by a warranty. A damaged or leaking medicine pack can be reported within 72 hours of delivery, with a photo of the pack.

## How to claim

Open the order, choose the item and select Report a problem. Keep the original packaging until the claim is confirmed.
