# Product warranty

## What is covered

Non-medicine health devices bought through CarePulse, such as thermometers and blood pressure monitors, carry a 12-month manufacturer warranty against defects.

## Medicines

Medicines are not covered by a warranty. A damaged or leaking medicine pack can be reported within 48 hours of delivery, with a photo of the pack.

## How to claim

Open the order, choose the item and select Report a problem. Keep the original packaging until the claim is confirmed.
