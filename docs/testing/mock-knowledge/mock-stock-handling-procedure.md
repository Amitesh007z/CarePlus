# Stock handling (staff only)

## Storage

Store cough syrups and tablets below 30 degrees Celsius, away from direct sunlight.

## Damaged packs

Move a damaged or leaking pack to the quarantine shelf. Do not sell it. Record the quarantine in the stock movement log with the reason.

## Expiry

Check expiry dates at each stock count. Remove expired packs from the shelf the same day and log them as write-offs.
