# Prescription refills

## How long is a prescription valid?

A digital prescription is valid for 30 days from the date it is issued.

## Can I refill a prescription?

No. A prescription can be used once. For a repeat medicine, ask your doctor for a new prescription through a consultation.

## Chronic medicines

Some long-term medicines, marked as chronic-eligible in the catalogue, can have a 90-day validity. Ask your doctor whether your medicine qualifies.
